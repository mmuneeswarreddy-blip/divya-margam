📖 About the Temple

History of Srikalahasteeswara Swamy Temple

Srikalahasteeswara Swamy Temple is one of the important Shaiva temples of South India. The temple is located in Srikalahasti, Andhra Pradesh, near the banks of the Swarnamukhi River.

The temple is traditionally associated with Lord Shiva worshipped as Srikalahasteeswara Swamy. It is revered as the Vayu Kshetra among the Pancha Bhoota Shiva Kshetras, representing the element of air.

Srikalahasti is also associated with the sacred stories of Sri, Kala and Hasti, represented by a spider, serpent and elephant, as well as the devotion of Bhakta Kannappa. These traditions have made the temple an important pilgrimage centre for devotees of Lord Shiva.
